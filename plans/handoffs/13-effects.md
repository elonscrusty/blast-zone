# 13 Combat effects polish
- Done: carter's pick "Sticker Punch" (chunky, navy outlines, gold/white stars, confetti, comic words, white clouds) for muzzle flash, tracers, hit flash, impacts, kill burst, damage numbers, slide dust + speed lines, crate hit/break, orb pickup (+XP), blast warning ring and blast, plus optional camera shake. Files: `src/client/Effects/`.
- Old effects were improved, not copied: `Combat/CombatFx.luau` keeps its calls and now delegates to `Effects/Fx.luau`; `PickupFxSystem` and `SlideTrail` call `Fx` too. `HealthBars.modelOf(id)` finds a fighter for the hit flash.
- Cheap by design: everything is pooled at start (stars, numbers, words, rings, tracers, particle holders). `FxBudget` caps particles (220), parts and numbers (`Config.Effects.caps`, tied to `Config.Perf`); above 70% busy a burst is halved. Nothing is made mid-fight. Opponents stay visible: effects are small, short (under 2.5 s) and low.
- Settings: Camera shake slider scales all shake and the field-of-view kick (0 = none). Fewer flashes drops strobing stars, halves big flashes, and skips the screen flash and softens the hit flash.
- Tuning knobs: all in `Config.Effects` (caps, shake, trauma per event, FOV kick, falloff, number size/time) and `EffectRecipes.luau` (what each effect is made of). Camera shake runs at render priority Camera+2, after `CameraSystem`.
- Tests: `tests/effects.test.luau` (budget caps and expiry, shake limits/decay/zero, recipes short and small, Fewer flashes, tier colors, config ties). 342 pass. stylua clean, rojo builds, every changed file compiles.
- NOT run in Studio: how it looks and feels, particle texture look, phone frame rate, SurfaceGui rings on real floors. Selene and luau-lsp type checks not run here (CI runs selene).
- Left out on purpose: hit-stop and character squash/knockback (not safe to do on the client). Particle outlines are not possible without art, so confetti and clouds use built-in textures; stars, words, rings and tracers carry the navy outline.
- Left: sounds (piece 12); kill bursts show only for your own kills (no kill position goes to others); mortar shell is still a plain gold ball. Judge first on a phone: kill burst, blast, slide.
