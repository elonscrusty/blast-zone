# AD Airdrop with a light beam (video 3 gap #3)
- Done: twice a match (`Config.Airdrop.count`) a supply crate falls into the circle. A tall light beam marks the spot for everyone, the crate lands after 7 s, stays shut 4 s (so fighters race there), then bursts open and the loot lies on the street: one found gun, 2 heal packs, 6 XP orbs. The beam fades out.
- Server decides everything: `src/server/Airdrop/AirdropSystem.luau` picks the times (`AirdropRules.schedule`, spread 45-115 s after the fight starts), the spot (random point inside the circle as it will be, raycast checked: open street, not a roof or prop, 40+ studs from the last drop) and the loot. It reuses `WeaponDrops.spawn`, `PickupSystem.spawnHeal` and `spawnOrbs`, so claiming is already server-checked. No new client ask except `AirdropRequest` (a late joiner asks for the running drops).
- Client: `AirdropClientSystem` draws the beam, falling crate and ground ring from the server's times (world art, listed in `tests/ui_tokens`); `AirdropHintSystem` shows a placeholder strip (words, seconds, arrow, distance) built with `Ui/Components` on the `Moments` layer. Claude Design owns the real strip.
- Looks: three beam looks live in `Config.Airdrop.looks` (A Gold Pillar, B Cyan Searchlight, C Coral Flare). `Config.Airdrop.look` picks one; the other two stay as skins. Preview: previews/airdrop/ in project files.
- Remotes: `AirdropState` (event incoming / opened / clear) and `AirdropRequest`, both owner "AD" in `RemoteDefs`.
- Pure maths + tests: `src/shared/Airdrop/AirdropRules.luau`, `tests/airdrop.test.luau` (11 cases). stylua, lune (1002 pass) and rojo build are clean; selene runs in CI.
- NOT tested until carter plays: raycast spots on each map, how the beam and crate look in the real night lighting, whether 2 drops and the timings feel right, the hint strip spot. All numbers are placeholders.
- Bots do not walk to airdrops on purpose. No sound (no sound ids made up).
