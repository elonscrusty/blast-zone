# Speed check for carter (play test)

Goal: **60 FPS on PC. 30 FPS on a weak phone.** 8 fighters (you + 7 bots).
Turn on Roblox "Performance Stats" (Settings menu, or Shift+F5 on PC) and watch FPS, ping and memory.

## Do this

1. **Full match, 8 fighters.** Press PLAY, tap "Add bots now". Play to the end.
   - [ ] FPS never below 60 (PC) / 30 (phone) for more than a second or two.
2. **Dense firing.** Hold fire the whole fight. Switch to the Arc Mortar and fire it a lot.
   - [ ] FPS holds. [ ] Shots and sparks stay smooth. [ ] No long freeze.
3. **Many orbs.** Break lots of crates. Let orbs pile up (up to 60).
   - [ ] FPS holds. [ ] Orbs still get picked up fast.
4. **Slide spam.** Slide every time it is ready for 30 seconds.
   - [ ] No jumping back. [ ] FPS holds.
5. **Five matches in a row.** Press PLAY again each time. Watch **memory** at the start and end of each match.
   - [ ] Memory does not keep climbing (a small rise in match 1 to 2 is fine).
   - [ ] Match 5 feels like match 1.
6. **Odd moments** (tell me what you see):
   - [ ] Leave during the countdown (use a second device or friend).
   - [ ] Die and watch the spectate view until the end.
   - [ ] Tap "Claim" on the results screen many times. You get paid once.
   - [ ] Zone closes while you stand outside. FPS holds.
7. **Phone.** Repeat step 1 and 2 on the weakest phone you have.
   - [ ] 30 FPS or better. [ ] Buttons answer fast.

## If something is bad

Tell me: which step, which device, the FPS number. A photo of Performance Stats is best.
